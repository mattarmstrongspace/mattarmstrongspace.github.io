Before the study tour begins, I want to take a moment to consider what I am hoping to gain from the experience, what I might bring to it, and what I would like to understand better by the time I return.

01 / PERSONAL, ACADEMIC & CULTURAL GOALS

Expectations and aspirations.

As I prepare for the Global Leadership Study Tour in Japan, I am very much looking forward to diving further into a culture that has already been a significant source of inspiration for me. Having already spent my last two months here, I’ve learnt a lot through my own personal kind of exploration, slowly traveling from Fukuoka to Tokyo, and am looking forward to furthering my learnings through an incredibly unique experience like this. Much of my early interest in Japan has been developed through Japanese role-playing games (JRPGs), music and other forms of entertainment. However, I recognize that these mediums provide such a limited perspective of Japanese society. One of my main goals for this tour is to move beyond those influences, and develop a more authentic understanding of the Japanese culture, its values, and the way that they are reflected in every day life.

02 / CULTURAL SENSITIVITY & LEADERSHIP

Representing more than myself.

An important aspect of this tour will be recognising my responsibility as a representative of my university and Australia. I intend to approach the experience with cultural sensitivity, curiosity, and an open mind. I understand that my existing perceptions of Japan may not always reflect the realities of Japanese society, and I believe it is important to remain receptive to perspectives that challenge my assumptions. From a global leadership perspective, I hope to develop my intercultural competence and adaptability by engaging respectfully with unfamiliar environments and learning from the people and experiences around me. I also recognise that cultural appreciation requires an understanding of context, particularly when drawing inspiration from cultures other than my own.

03 / A QUESTION TO EXPLORE

Looking at the experience through my field.

One theme I would particularly like to explore during the tour is the relationship between cultural identity, music, and storytelling in Japanese video game design. I am interested in how traditional cultural influences can be incorporated into contemporary creative industries, and how these influences contribute to the identity and emotional impact of a game. An international perspective could help me better understand how cultural experiences shape creative decisions and how developers can responsibly represent cultural influences in their work. This is particularly relevant to my future aspirations, as I hope to create work that is both creatively engaging and informed by genuine cultural understanding.

04 / A NOTE TO MY FUTURE SELF

What I hope to bring home.

Looking towards my future self after the tour, I hope to have gained more than just memorable experiences. I want to return with a broader worldview, a deeper appreciation of Japanese culture, and new perspectives that I can apply to both my studies and professional development. I also hope to become more confident in adapting to unfamiliar situations and approaching challenges with greater creativity. Throughout the tour, I intend to reflect on my experiences through journaling, particularly moments that challenge my expectations or provide new insight. Ultimately, I hope this experience will influence not only the kind of developer and musician I become, but also the way I engage with different cultures and perspectives in the future.
