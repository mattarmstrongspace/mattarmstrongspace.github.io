THE APPROACH — 001

Personal experience.
Wider understanding.

I'm documenting an international study experience through personal observations, cultural reflection and research into Japanese society.

This journal brings together the personal and the academic. Each entry begins with an experience or observation, considers why it matters, and connects it with wider ideas about culture, communication, society and global leadership.

The aim isn't simply to describe what happened, but to reflect on what I learned, question my assumptions and consider how an international perspective can change the way I see the world.
